# Code of conduct

Tahoe Intelligence is made in the open. Everyone who takes part, in [issues](https://github.com/krishnanefx/obsidian-tahoe-intelligence/issues), [Discussions](https://github.com/krishnanefx/obsidian-tahoe-intelligence/discussions) and pull requests, should be able to do so without being harassed or put down.

## What's expected

- Be kind, and assume good faith: most people asking a question or reporting a bug are trying to help.
- Talk about the work, not the person.
- A no to an idea isn't a judgement of you, and a question is never too basic to ask.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal or political attacks.
- Demeaning anyone for who they are: their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, nationality, appearance, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or email, without their permission.
- Spam, trolling, and repeatedly derailing a conversation.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's own reporting: **Report content** in the ··· menu of any comment, issue, pull request or discussion, or **Block or report** on the person's profile. Reports go to GitHub's Trust & Safety team. See [Reporting abuse or spam](https://docs.github.com/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

## What happens

Comments, issues and pull requests that break this code may be edited, hidden or deleted, threads locked, and the people who keep breaking it blocked from the repository.
